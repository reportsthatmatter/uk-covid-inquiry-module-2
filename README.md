# UK Covid-19 Inquiry: Modules 2, 2A, 2B, 2C report

HC 1436, presented to Parliament on 20 November 2025 under section 26 of the Inquiries Act 2005 (published on the Inquiry's website the same day). The UK Covid-19 Inquiry's second report: core UK decision-making and political governance, drawing on Module 2 (UK), 2A (Scotland), 2B (Wales) and 2C (Northern Ireland), by the Chair, the Rt Hon the Baroness Hallett DBE. Two volumes (460 and 336 PDF pages).

## Scope

Both volumes, as the report id `uk-covid-inquiry-module-2`, in the order the Inquiry numbers them:

- **Volume I** (HC 1436-I, Introduction and key events from January 2020 to May 2022; 460 PDF pages): the Chair's Introduction, Voices, the Executive summary (which summarises the whole report's findings and lists the recommendations in brief), and Chapters 1 to 8, the account of key events.
- **Volume II** (HC 1436-II, Key themes, lessons and recommendations; 336 PDF pages): Voices, Chapters 9 to 15 (scientific and technical advice; vulnerabilities and inequalities; government decision-making; public health communications; legislation and enforcement; intergovernmental working; key lessons for future emergencies), Appendix 1 (the background to the module and the Inquiry's methodology), Appendix 2 (glossary) and Appendix 3, the full list of the 19 recommendations. Added 12 November 2026 (reportsthatmatter-nshl).

Volume I went first (October 2026) because it carries the Introduction and the Executive summary, where the report's headline findings are stated, and the chronology the press quoted most. The Inquiry's other module reports are separate reports with their own ids. The In Brief summary, the Easy Read edition and translations are separate publications and out of scope.
